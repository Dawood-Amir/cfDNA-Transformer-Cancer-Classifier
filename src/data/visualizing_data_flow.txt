        │
        ▼
  Preprocessing Script (THIS FILE)
        │
        ├── For each region:
        │   ├── Merge all fragment tokens into one list
        │   ├── Tokenize with <cls> and <sep>
        │   └── Store as flat "tokens" list
        │
        ▼
  .pt Files (flat regions, no fragment hierarchy)
        │
        ▼
  PatientDataset (loads .pt files)
        │
        ▼
  PatientCollate (pads to batch size)
        │
        ▼
  RegionEncoder (processes regions)
        │
        ▼
  RegionTransformer (processes tokens)
